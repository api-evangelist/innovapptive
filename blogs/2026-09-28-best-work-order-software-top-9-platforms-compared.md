Selecting the best work order software is a different decision for a large industrial plant than for a small maintenance team. At that scale, a work order system is effective only if it connects seamlessly to the systems you already run, such as SAP, IBM Maximo, or Oracle, and reaches technicians in the field even when connectivity is limited. This guide compares 9 work order software platforms through an asset-intensive operations lens, including oil and gas, chemicals, utilities, mining, and manufacturing.
