Asset reliability is often the most quoted number in a plant, and often the least trusted. A critical pump or compressor can show ninety-nine percent availability on the morning dashboard and still trip every few weeks, driving overtime, contractor callouts, and a maintenance budget that never behaves. The number on the reliability report and the failure history behind it are frequently two different things.
