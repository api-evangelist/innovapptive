Most storeroom inaccuracy doesn't announce itself. It shows up as a work order stuck in parts waiting for days, a bearing reordered twice because no one trusted the ERP count, or a technician hunting the wrong bin while a heat exchanger sits idle. Inventory accuracy is the percentage of your physical stock that matches what your ERP system records.
