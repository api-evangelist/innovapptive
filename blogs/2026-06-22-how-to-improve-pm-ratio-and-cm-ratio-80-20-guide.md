Fixing your PM ratio is one of the highest-return moves a maintenance team can make. Emergency repairs cost 3 to 10 times more than the same job done on a schedule, and most industrial plants are still running at less than half the planned maintenance target. The industry benchmark is 80% planned work, 20% corrective or reactive work.
