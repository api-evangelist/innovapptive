A plant can have every role filled and still experience a manufacturing skills gap. Very often, open jobs are the visible part of the problem, while the most expensive issues show up on fully staffed shifts, where jobs take longer, wait for the one person who knows the asset, or go to an outside contractor. This guide treats the skills gap in manufacturing as an operating cost problem rather than only a hiring problem.
