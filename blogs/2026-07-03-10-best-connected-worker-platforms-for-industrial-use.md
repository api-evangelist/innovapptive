Connected worker platforms have evolved beyond digital forms and mobile inspections. Today, industrial organizations need platforms that execute maintenance, operations, inventory, and safety workflows reliably across thousands of frontline workers. While dozens of vendors claim to connect the frontline, far fewer can support complex plants running SAP or IBM Maximo, operating in hazardous environments, and managing multi-million-dollar maintenance budgets.
