When work orders pile up faster than your team can close them, you have a maintenance backlog. More precisely, it is the queue of approved maintenance tasks, planned and unplanned, that have not been completed yet. Left unmanaged, a growing backlog drives unplanned downtime , higher reactive maintenance costs, and missed compliance windows.
